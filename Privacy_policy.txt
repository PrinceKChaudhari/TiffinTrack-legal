TiffinTrack Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

TiffinTrack is a Telegram-based order management system designed for tiffin services and home food businesses. This Privacy Policy explains how user data is collected, used, and protected when using the bot.

1. Information We Collect



TiffinTrack collects only the minimum information required for the service to function:

Telegram User ID

Telegram Name and Username (if available)

Customer order responses (Confirm or Cancel)

Menu data created by operators

Order history generated through interactions

Payment status information marked by operators


We do not collect passwords, banking details, or sensitive personal information.

2. How We Use Information



The collected data is used only for operational purposes:

Managing daily menus published by operators

Recording customer order confirmations and cancellations

Generating daily reports for operators

Tracking payment status (paid/unpaid)

Ensuring proper functioning of the bot system


We do not use data for advertising, profiling, or marketing.

3. Data Storage



All data is stored in a local JSON-based storage system controlled by the bot deployment.

No external database services are used

No third-party analytics tools are involved

Data remains within the bot’s hosting environment


Each operator’s data is logically separated inside the system.

4. Data Sharing



We do not sell, rent, or share user data with any third parties.

Data is only accessible to:

The operator managing the tiffin service

The customer interacting through Telegram


No external organization has access to stored data.

5. Data Security



We use basic technical measures to protect data:

Atomic file writes to prevent corruption

Event logging for tracking changes

Locking mechanisms to avoid concurrent write issues

Recovery system for corrupted data states


However, no system is completely secure.

6. User Rights



Users may:

Stop using the service at any time

Request removal of their data through the operator

Avoid interacting with the bot if they disagree with this policy


Operators control customer data within their instance.

7. Third-Party Services



TiffinTrack operates inside Telegram.

We follow Telegram’s infrastructure and policies.

We do not use third-party advertising or analytics services.

8. Children’s Privacy



TiffinTrack is not intended for independent use by children under 13 years of age without supervision.

We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to This Policy



This policy may be updated in the future.

Updates will be reflected by changing the “Last updated” date.

10. Contact



For any privacy concerns, contact the bot operator via Telegram.
